I'm Mike Olivieri. A dad who likes basketball and coding, and the person behind **hoopdad dunks**.

This is my working notebook for the agent era: original thinking, practical experiments, and the lessons that emerge when you actually build things.

## What you'll find here

**Agents** explores architecture, orchestration, communication, and the operating models that turn capability into useful outcomes.

**AI Engineering** covers implementation, grounding, and the guardrails needed to move from a compelling demonstration to something reliable.

**Tokenomics** looks at the economics of AI tokens: efficiency, feedback loops, energy, and the architectural decisions behind the cost.

The earlier **Cloud Architecture** and **Data Engineering** work stays here too. Good agent systems still depend on solid foundations.
